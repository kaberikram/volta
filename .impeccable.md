## Design Context

### Users
Someone on desktop Chrome turning a clip and a song into a beat-locked dot edit, then exporting an MP4. They are waiting on analysis and judging the picture, not filling out a form.

### Brand Personality
Tactile, rhythmic, friendly. Fun comes from the picture and the beat grid, not from decoration. The voice is short and plain.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark mono docked editor. Panels sit edge to edge, split by 1px hairlines, and the viewer is the darkest well. No hue in the chrome. White is the rare accent: the playhead, the section box, and whichever action is next.

Anti-references: the light floating-card studio, the old cramped dark form, and generic bold UI (glass, gradient text, purple glow, side-stripes).

### Design Principles
- The stage is the hero. Controls stay small and out of its way.
- Group by job, not by step number.
- One white action at a time.
- One screen; panels scroll inside, never the page.
- Keep every existing control id and the analysis, plan, and export pipeline intact.
